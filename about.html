About NextGen Robotics

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit giving youth hands-on STEAM opportunities through FIRST LEGO League.

Gear Up, our FLL Challenge team (608), has been building robots since 2014. In 2025, that decade of experience grew into NextGen Robotics: a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded to go beyond a single team and open STEAM opportunities to more of our community. Great Gears, our FLL Explore team (33994), launched alongside it.

Mission

To provide opportunities for youth to develop problem solving, critical thinking, creativity, and innovative ideas using science, technology, engineering, artistic, and math (STEAM) skills in a fun, hands-on, collaborative environment.

Vision

A community where every youth has the opportunity to explore and develop technical and artistic skills, teamwork, community engagement, and leadership.
